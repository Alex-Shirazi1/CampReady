# App Store listing

**Name:** CampReady
**Subtitle:** Know what to expect before you camp
**Category:** Travel
**Price:** Free, with CampReady Plus at $2.99/month

## Description

Your next weekend, well prepared.

CampReady puts everything you check before a camping trip in one place: the campsite, a live weather forecast for your dates, fire rules and trail access. Plan the trip, invite your group, and everyone hears about it when something changes.

**Find a campsite near you**
Turn on location to see campsites sorted by distance, or search by city or park. Filter by tent or RV, group size, budget and pets.

**Know the conditions**
See a live forecast for your trip dates, a packing tip for the weather, and the fire rules and road access around the site.

**Plan together**
Pick your dates, request the booking, and invite friends by email. They see the plan, accept or decline, and you see who's coming.

**Never miss a change**
Get a push notification when you're invited, when the dates change, or when someone answers your invite. Tap it to jump straight to the trip.

**CampReady Plus**
Plan more than one trip at a time and save trip summaries for places without reception.
